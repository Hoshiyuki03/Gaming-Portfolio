# Katha Legends – Deep Dive

## World

### Elyria – Realm of Ancients

Elyria is an ancient world consisting of multiple kingdoms, forgotten civilizations, hidden relics, and conflicts that shape the future of its people.

The world is not divided into simple heroes and villains.

Humans and demons both possess their own cultures, motivations, histories, and ambitions.

Players gradually uncover hidden truths while exploring a world filled with mystery, danger, and discovery.

---

## Story Overview

The journey follows a protagonist who sets out to explore the world with little knowledge of the people, kingdoms, and conflicts around them.

As the adventure unfolds, ancient secrets begin to surface.

The greatest mystery is not the player.

A hidden prophecy surrounds the player's mother and a truth she has kept hidden for years.

The player's actions influence how this story unfolds and what parts of the world's history become known.

---

## Core Features

### Weapon Spirit System

Weapons are more than equipment.

Each weapon possesses a spirit with its own history, legacy, and connection to the ancient world.

Obtaining a weapon means inheriting part of its story.

---

### Relic Fall Event

A large-scale world event where players compete and cooperate to obtain powerful ancient relics.

Success depends on strategy, teamwork, preparation, and timing.

Every Relic Fall has the potential to create memorable player-driven stories.

---

### Cooperative Dungeons

Dungeons are designed around:

- Communication
- Trust
- Puzzle Solving
- Exploration
- Team Coordination

Many dungeons contain hidden mysteries that reveal themselves based on player actions rather than simply defeating a boss.

---

### Multiple Kingdoms

Elyria contains numerous kingdoms, cultures, and factions.

Each possesses its own history, politics, and conflicts.

Players encounter both allies and enemies across the world while uncovering connections between kingdoms and ancient events.

---

### Meaningful Choices

Player decisions affect:

- Relationships
- Quest Outcomes
- Discoveries
- Story Development
- World Understanding

Choices are intended to feel meaningful rather than cosmetic.

---

### Exploration

Exploration is a central pillar of the experience.

Hidden locations, forgotten ruins, secret lore, and rare discoveries reward players who choose to venture beyond the main path.

---

## Design Goals

### Meaningful Exploration

Reward curiosity and discovery rather than simple checklist progression.

---

### Player Cooperation

Encourage teamwork through mechanics that require communication and trust.

---

### Story Consequences

Create choices that have lasting effects on player experiences.

---

### Living Fantasy World

Build a believable fantasy world filled with history, culture, mysteries, and evolving stories.

# Design Process

## 1. Idea

Every project begins with identifying an experience I want players to feel.

The goal is to define emotions, engagement, and memorable moments before designing mechanics.

---

## 2. Research

I study existing games, systems, player behavior, and design solutions to understand what works and where improvements can be made.

---

## 3. Worldbuilding

Lore, environments, factions, characters, and narrative foundations are developed to create a believable setting.

---

## 4. Gameplay Design

Core mechanics and systems are designed to support the intended player experience and project goals.

---

## 5. Prototype

Playable prototypes are created to validate ideas quickly and identify strengths and weaknesses.

---

## 6. Testing

Player feedback and observation help reveal usability issues and opportunities for improvement.

---

## 7. Iteration

Systems are refined repeatedly based on testing results to improve quality, clarity, and player enjoyment.

---

# Creative Philosophy

I believe great games are built around experiences rather than features.

Players remember:

- Discoveries
- Friendships
- Challenges
- Choices
- Mysteries
- Stories

More than statistics or progression systems.

My goal is to create worlds that players genuinely want to explore and remember long after they stop playing.
